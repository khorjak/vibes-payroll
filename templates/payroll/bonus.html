Bonus Run

Pay a bonus to any number of employees in one pay run, separate from regular pay. Federal tax is withheld at the flat 22% supplemental rate and Oklahoma tax at the year's top rate, for employees who had that tax withheld from regular pay this year or last; for others the bonus is taxed together with their latest regular paycheck. Only percent-of-pay benefit deductions (such as a 401k) and percent garnishments come out; fixed amounts are skipped.
