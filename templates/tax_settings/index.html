Saving an existing year replaces its figures. Draft paychecks pick up a change when recalculated; approved and paid paychecks keep what they were calculated with.

Where to find them

Social Security: the SSA "contribution and benefit base", announced each October for the next year (ssa.gov).

Federal withholding tables: IRS Publication 15-T, "Annual Percentage Method Tables" for automated payroll, published each December (irs.gov). After saving a year's wage bases, use its Enter tables link.

Oklahoma SUTA: the "Taxable Wage Base" on the OESC's Important Numbers sheet for the year, published each winter (oklahoma.gov/oesc).
